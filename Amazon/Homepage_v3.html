Reality check

What "I'll do it myself" actually looks like

These are the stories we hear regularly from store owners who get in touch — usually after spending 3–6 months trying to figure it out.

Pet accessories — Melbourne

6 months in. Still not live.

Started with a basic Seller Central setup. Got stuck on GTIN requirements for 3 weeks. Hired a VA to push through it — the VA got the product categories wrong, listings got suppressed. The appeal took another 4 weeks. By the time it was sorted, the original window was gone.

Every step in Amazon setup links to the next. A wrong category choice doesn't just affect one listing — it affects how PPC campaigns run, which keywords you're eligible for, and your account health score.

Supplements — Brisbane

Account suspended during verification.

Submitted the wrong document type during identity verification. Account suspended. Filed an appeal — Amazon responded in 8 weeks. Reinstated, but a competitor had accumulated 200+ reviews and owned the top spot for their primary keywords.

Suspensions during setup are common for new sellers who don't know exactly which documents Amazon requires. We've done this enough times to know what Amazon will and won't accept before we submit.

Homewares — Sydney

Live in 30 days. Unprofitable in 60.

Got products live without modelling the FBA fee impact. First month: $4,200 in revenue, $4,900 in FBA fees, referral fees, and storage charges combined. Had to pull all stock, reconfigure fulfilment, and relaunch — losing ranking and velocity in the process.

FBA is usually the right choice — but only after you've run the numbers. A product that sells for $28 with $6.50 FBA fees and 12% referral fees leaves very little margin to work with.

FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between TX Amazon Launch and TX Marketplace Accelerator? +
TX Amazon Launch is a one-time setup. We get your account created and products live in 30 days — you take it from there. TX Marketplace Accelerator includes the launch plus ongoing management: listing optimisation, PPC, A+ Content, FBA monitoring, and eventually eBay and Catch expansion. I already have an online store. Do I need to change anything? +
No. Amazon runs alongside your existing store, not instead of it. Amazon finds buyers who go directly to the platform — it doesn't compete with your current acquisition channels. Your store stays as it is. Amazon becomes an additional revenue channel that reaches a different kind of buyer.
What if I already have an Amazon seller account? +
We can work with an existing account. We audit it first to identify any compliance issues, listing problems, or account health flags before we build on top of it. Existing accounts with issues sometimes take longer to remediate than starting fresh — we'll assess and advise.
How does the 30-day guarantee work? +
TX Amazon Launch guarantees your products are listed on Amazon AU within 30 days of kickoff, or we refund your $2,500. TX Marketplace Accelerator guarantees your first sale within 30 days of going live, or your first month's management fee is refunded. What products don't work on Amazon AU? +
Categories with heavy regulatory requirements, restricted listings, or very low AU search volume need case-by-case assessment. We flag these before we start. We won't take your fee if your product category isn't viable.
