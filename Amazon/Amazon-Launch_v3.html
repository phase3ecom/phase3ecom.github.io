Why it's harder than it looks

Where DIY Amazon setup breaks down

Amazon Seller Central is built for scale, not for first-time sellers. Every step has a dependency. Get one wrong, and it creates a problem two steps later that's twice as hard to fix.

🪪

Account verification

Takes days and requires specific documentation — business registration, bank statements, ID — in formats Amazon accepts. Getting this wrong delays launch and triggers account flags that are hard to resolve.

🔢

GTINs (barcodes)

Required for most listings. Many AU store owners don't have GS1-registered GTINs. Getting exemptions or sourcing correctly is a process most sellers figure out the hard way — and often get wrong the first time.

⚖

Category & compliance

Varies by product type. Health and beauty may need ARTG registration. Electronics need AU compliance markings. Getting categorised incorrectly triggers listing suppression or account health warnings.

🚚

FBA configuration

Choosing FBA vs FBM has direct implications for margins, Prime eligibility, and Buy Box win rate. Most new sellers choose without modelling the numbers — and spend months undoing the mistake.

The result: a half-built account, or a live listing that's technically wrong and costs more to fix later than it would have cost to build correctly in the first place.
