02  /  Strategy
📊 What is RFM?

RFM stands for Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value. It's a method of scoring every customer on your email list based on how they've actually behaved — not just whether they're subscribed. Instead of sending every customer the same email, RFM lets us send the right email to the right person at the right moment. The result: higher open rates, more revenue per send, and fewer people unsubscribing because they're getting irrelevant content.

R
Recency
When did they last buy?

A customer who bought 2 weeks ago is far more likely to open an email than one who bought 8 months ago.
×
F
Frequency
How many times have they bought?

A 3x buyer behaves completely differently to a 1x buyer — they need different messaging.
×
M
Monetary
How much have they spent?

High-value customers deserve recognition. A $300 LTV customer shouldn't get the same email as a $30 one.

Customer Lifecycle
🔄 How Flows Move Customers Up the Ladder

Every flow we build targets a specific RFM transition. The goal is always to move a customer to a higher-value segment, or rescue them before they lapse. No single email goes to everyone. Each one is triggered by behaviour: a purchase, a gap in purchases, browsing without buying, or silence for 60+ days.

The growth path (top row) is where we want every customer to end up. Welcome and post-purchase flows do the heavy lifting early. The recovery path (bottom row) catches customers who go quiet. The win-back flow activates automatically at Day 60 with a soft check-in, escalates to social proof at Day 90, offers 15% off at Day 120, and sunset removes non-responders at Day 150 to keep the list healthy.

04  /  Paid Media
📱 Meta + Google

The biggest lever in paid advertising isn't budget — it's creative. Ads fatigue in 4-6 weeks. The same image running month after month drives up costs and kills performance. We refresh the creative every month so we're always testing fresh angles against what's working.

Monthly Process
🔄 How Each Month Works
1
📊 Review performance
Identify which creatives are fatiguing — declining CTR, rising CPM, frequency above 3x. Pull top performers to build on. Flag what to retire.
2
✏️ Brief new concepts
3-5 new ad concepts based on what's working and which angles haven't been tested. Hooks always come from real customer language, not marketing speak.
3
🎬 Produce assets
2-3 short videos (15-30 sec) plus 2-3 statics. All sized for Meta (1x1, 4x5, 9x16) and Google responsive display. Using LAVIO's own product photography plus UGC where available.
4
🚀 Upload + test
New creative added alongside existing campaigns — no rebuilds needed. Meta's algorithm allocates budget automatically to the best performers.
5
🗑️ Retire what's not working
Lean ad sets (fewer creatives per set) learn faster and spend more efficiently. Remove anything that hasn't found traction after 2+ weeks of data.
