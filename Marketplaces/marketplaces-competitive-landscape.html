The Market Gap
No competitor is targeting the Shopify/WooCommerce seller who has never tried Amazon — with an accessible, fixed price.
Who All Five Competitors Target

All five competitors go after brands already on Amazon who want to scale, OR enterprise/mid-market brands building Amazon from scratch with big budgets. Phase 3 owns the gap between ‘interested but overwhelmed’ and ‘ready to commit to a $3–5k/month agency.’

Phase 3 Key Advantages
ADVANTAGE 01
Entry Price Point
$1–2k setup + $1k/month retainer is a fundamentally different price point to any competitor in the market.
ADVANTAGE 02
Existing Trust
Phase 3 already has trust with Shopify store owners — none of these competitors do.
ADVANTAGE 03
Additive Channel Positioning
Amazon becomes additive to an existing cross-channel growth relationship (Shopify + Klaviyo + Google + Meta + Amazon). No competitor positions Amazon as a Shopify growth channel — they all position it as a separate, competing channel.
