Usage Notes
01
⚠ Before Running These Scripts — Read First
  • Scripts 1 and 2: cold-to-warm audiences (no prior brand exposure)
  • Script 3: warm/retargeting audiences (have seen landing page or prior content)
  • Do NOT run retargeting copy to cold audiences — suppresses conversion and misleads CPL data
  • Separate ad sets, separate creative, separate KPIs for each script
  • Publish Script 1 first. Test spend before running all three simultaneously.

Script 1 — The $6,700 Math
02
Script 1 — The $6,700 Math
Angle: Cost comparison (Angle A)
Objective: Kill the “I’ll do it myself” objection
Audience Shopify brand owners, AU, $500k–$2M revenue, cold
Format 30 sec · Direct to camera · No logo intro · No music
Placement Works in story + feed
Objective Kill the “I’ll do it myself” objection before it forms
[Direct to camera, founder-style, no logo intro] “If you’re thinking about launching on Amazon yourself — here’s what that actually costs. Account setup: $200 and 3 hours. Catalogue build: 8–12 hours of your time. Product copy: $400–$600 per SKU if you outsource it. Photography reformatting: $200. And then there’s the first 90 days of figuring out what you got wrong. Total? Most brands spend $6,700 before they make a dollar. We charge $1,500 and a 30-day guarantee. Your products are live or your money back. Link in bio.”
Production Notes
  • No B-roll required
  • Can use simple cost breakdown graphic as lower-third or cut-in
  • Founder-style delivery preferred (not polished presenter)
  • End card: “30-day launch. $1,500. Refund guarantee. Apply at [URL]”

Script 2 — Reviews Compound

Script 2 — Reviews Compound
Angle: Reviews compound like interest (Angle B)
Objective: Make procrastination cost visible and urgent
Audience Shopify brand owners, AU, already running ads, growth mindset, cold-to-warm
Format 35 sec · Hook frame + VO or direct to camera · Can use B-roll
Placement Feed preferred
Objective Make procrastination cost visible and urgent
[Hook frame: static text overlay — “Your competitor has 200 Amazon reviews. You have zero.”] [VO or direct to camera:] “Amazon’s algorithm is a flywheel. Reviews drive ranking. Ranking drives visibility. Visibility drives sales, which drive more reviews. Every month you’re not on Amazon, that flywheel spins for your competitor, not you. The brand that gets there first isn’t just ahead — they’re compounding. That gap doesn’t close easily. We launch you on Amazon in 30 days. The flywheel starts turning. Link in bio.”
Production Notes
  • Can use competitor review count screenshots as B-roll if available
  • Hook frame is critical — must hold for at least 2 seconds
  • End card: “30-day launch. $1,500. Refund guarantee. Apply at [URL]”

Script 3 — Identity Gap

Script 3 — Identity Gap
Angle: Identity gap (Angle E)
Audience: WARM / retargeting only
Audience Shopify brand owners, AU, warm/retargeting (assume some brand awareness)
Format 40 sec · Direct to camera · Hook frame
Placement Feed + stories
Objective Reframe Amazon as complementary channel, not competitor; broad reach
[Hook frame: “You sell on Shopify. Your competitor sells on Shopify and Amazon.”] [Direct to camera:] “I’m not saying Amazon is better than Shopify. I’m saying they’re not competing with each other. Amazon is where people go when they know what they want and they want it now. Shopify is where your brand lives. If you’re only on Shopify, you’re invisible to every buyer who starts their search on Amazon. That’s about 30% of all product searches in AU. You don’t have to choose. You just have to show up. 30-day launch. $1,500. Refund guarantee. Link in bio.”
Production Notes
  • Works for retargeting — assumes some prior brand awareness
  • Keep hook frame visible for 2–3 seconds before cutting to camera
  • End card: “30-day launch. $1,500. Refund guarantee. Apply at [URL]”
  • Debate-triggering angle — expect comment engagement; do not respond defensively
05
Do Not Do
05
✗ Do not lead any script with the guarantee — it’s a trust closer, not a hook
✗ Do not mention “other marketplaces” without naming them (eBay AU, Catch)
✗ Do not run Script 3 to cold audiences — requires prior brand awareness to land
