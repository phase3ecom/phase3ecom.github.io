Full Offer Architecture

Five natural service tiers mapped to the AU DTC seller journey. Current offer sits at Tier 2. Deploy in sequence as internal capacity allows.

Tier Offer Name Purpose Price Signal
1 Amazon AU Readiness Audit Lead generation + qualification. Paid diagnostic — audit output becomes the proposal. $500–$750
2 Amazon AU Launch Sprint (current) First paid engagement. Fixed scope, 30–45 days, guaranteed delivery. $1,500 setup
3 Amazon Profit Engine Ongoing growth. PPC management, listing optimisation, weekly margin reporting. $1,000/month
4 Amazon Rescue Desk High-urgency reactive work. Stuck listings, compliance escalation, reimbursement recovery. $750–$1,500/case
5 Fractional Head of Marketplace Senior strategy for 7–8 figure brands without a full-time internal Amazon team. TBC
Strongest AU market gap: Rescue Desk. Every competitor sells PPC and listing optimisation. Nobody sells compliance rescue, support escalation, stuck listing triage, or reimbursement recovery. Founders pay premium for relief from operational dread. Least glamorous, most defensible position.
Category 1

Risk-Reversal Offers

Alternatives to the 30-day delivery guarantee. Replace or layer on top of the current offer.

First Month Free
Pay $1,500 setup only. Retainer starts Month 2.
Why it works
Removes "paying before I see results" objection. Lower upfront commitment. Client sees value before cash outlay.
Risk
Acquires lower-intent buyers. Month 2 churn risk if onboarding not tight.
90-Day Results Pilot
Fixed 90-day engagement. KPIs defined at kickoff (live listings, indexed, first reviews). Miss KPIs — Month 3 is free.
Why it works
Replaces open-ended retainer with a structured trial. Reduces "how do I exit?" anxiety for margin-anxious buyers.
Risk
KPI definition requires care — set too easy or too hard. Needs internal SLA documentation.
Full Refund — Setup + Month 1
Full $2,500 refund if not live and ranked within 60 days.
Why it works
Stronger confidence signal than delivery guarantee alone. Elevates positioning above any competitor with a weaker guarantee.
Risk
Higher financial exposure. Needs tight internal SLA. Not viable until consistent 30-day delivery is confirmed.
We Cover the GTINs
Phase 3 Ecom pays for GS1 barcode registration (~$175). Deducted from setup if client stays 3+ months.
Why it works
Removes a known friction point in the setup process. Small dollar amount, high perceived value — feels like we own the process.
Risk
Upfront cost absorbed by agency. Needs to be factored into unit economics from Day 1.
Category 2

Acquisition Offers

Low-barrier entry points designed to start the relationship before the setup fee conversation.

Free Amazon Brand Audit
1–2 hr review: competitor listings, search visibility gaps, ASIN opportunities, estimated revenue uplift. No charge.
Why it works
Prospect sees their own specific gap in real time — no persuasion required. Converts to proposal from a warm, informed position.
Risk
Time-intensive. Must be templated to scale. Best suited for existing Phase 3 clients first.
Free First Listing
We list one hero product at no charge. Setup fee applies from Product 2 onward.
Why it works
Proof of execution before commitment. Removes "I don't know what I'm paying for" objection with a tangible deliverable.
Risk
Labour cost on speculative work. First listing quality must be excellent — sets the expectation for everything that follows.
Founding Client Rate Lock
First 10 clients lock the $1,500/$1,000 rate permanently. Price rises after launch.
Why it works
Creates urgency without discount. Rewards early adopters. Price rise framing positions the agency as growing in demand.
Risk
Rate lock must be honoured indefinitely — model risk if operational costs rise. Define exact terms in writing.
Free Competitor Analysis
PDF report showing exactly what the prospect's top 3 competitors are doing on Amazon: listing quality, reviews, pricing, keywords.
Why it works
Threat framing — prospect sees what they're losing without having to imagine it. Works well with Identity Gap and Invisible Customer angles.
Risk
Commodity risk if competitors copy the format. Template carefully and brand it distinctly.
Category 3

Pricing Structure Alternatives

Different ways to package the same service. Some expand the addressable market; others change the risk profile.

No Setup Fee
$0 setup. Retainer starts at $1,500/month for the first 3 months, then drops to $1,000.
Why it works
Eliminates upfront cost objection entirely. Smooths cash flow for time-poor clients who resist large single payments.
Risk
Higher total cost to client — needs clear communication upfront. Total 3-month spend is $4,500 vs $3,500 current.
Setup Only (No Retainer)
$2,500 flat. Full setup, no ongoing management. Structured handoff doc included.
Why it works
Captures the DIY segment. Lower LTV but larger addressable market — especially Audience 1 who are self-sufficient.
Risk
Poorly managed Amazon account post-handoff can reflect on agency. Needs a clean exit process and clear scope of responsibility.
Revenue Share
$500 setup + 10–15% of Amazon revenue monthly. No fixed retainer.
Why it works
Aligns incentives entirely. Eliminates "paying $1k/month and nothing's happening" churn trigger. Strongest confidence signal of all options.
Risk
Requires revenue reporting access. Hard to audit without Shopify/Amazon data integration. Not viable without 6+ months of client data to model risk.
Tiered Retainer
Month 1–3: $1,500/month (setup + PPC launch). Month 4–6: $1,000/month (optimisation). Month 7+: $750/month (maintenance).
Why it works
Maps price to value phase. Easier to justify at each stage — client understands they're paying more when the work is heaviest.
Risk
More complex to communicate and invoice. Month 7+ rate may undervalue ongoing management if PPC spend grows.
Product-Volume Pricing
$1,500 setup covers up to 10 SKUs. $500 per additional 10 SKUs.
Why it works
Fair for large catalogues. Increases setup revenue from complex accounts without punishing small-catalogue clients.
Risk
Requires clear SKU count definition upfront. Scope creep risk if "SKU" means different things to client vs agency.
Category 4

Value-Add Offers

Same price point, more perceived value. Layer onto the current offer without changing the fee structure.

Amazon Ads Credit Match
Phase 3 Ecom matches the client's first $200 of Amazon ad spend.
Why it works
Removes "what's my ad budget on top of the retainer?" friction. A concrete dollar value that feels like partnership, not just service delivery.
Risk
~$200 cost per acquisition. Small at current volume; material at scale. Needs to be built into pricing model.
90-Day Review Roadmap
Structured post-launch plan delivered at kickoff: Week 1–4 setup milestones, Days 30/60/90 check-ins with metrics review.
Why it works
Reduces post-purchase anxiety. Client knows exactly what happens next — critical for Audience 1 (time-poor, margin-anxious).
Risk
Creates accountability — roadmap must be delivered as specified. Build the template before selling it.
Marketplace Expansion Included
Setup includes Amazon + one additional marketplace (eBay AU, Catch, or Kogan).
Why it works
Broadens the value proposition beyond Amazon. Differentiates from Amazon-only competitors. Maps directly to the "40% of AU sales on marketplaces" stat.
Risk
Adds operational complexity. Only viable once Amazon setup process is fully templated and consistent.
Dedicated Slack Channel
Client gets a shared Slack channel with direct async access to the team during setup and first 90 days.
Why it works
Perceived intimacy and responsiveness. Reduces support emails. Especially valuable for Audience 1 who are anxious about handing over control.
Risk
Low direct cost. Primary risk is scope creep in channel conversations. Define the channel's purpose clearly at kickoff.
New Offer — Tier 1

Amazon AU Readiness Audit

Paid diagnostic. Strongest lead generation and qualification mechanism from AU market research. Audit output becomes the proposal — no persuasion required.

Scope
SKU selection and margin modelling, fee + PPC scenario planning, pricing and Buy Box risk review, competitor shelf scan, launch roadmap
Price
$500–$750 (or free for existing Phase 3 Ecom clients as upsell entry point)
Turnaround
1–2 business days
Converts to
Launch Sprint (Tier 2) or Profit Engine retainer (Tier 3)
Why it works
Prospect sees their own specific gap in real time — no persuasion needed. The audit output is the proposal. Qualifies intent before the setup fee is ever mentioned.
Risk
Time-intensive until templated. Must systematise the audit format before scaling. Do not promote this offer without a repeatable delivery process.
New Offer — Tier 4

Amazon Rescue Desk

Most underserved segment in AU. Competitors don't touch this. High urgency = high willingness to pay. Acquired via organic search — no paid ads required.

Scope
Stuck or inactive listings, compliance case escalation, billing lockouts, reimbursement disputes, account health triage, appeal writing
Price
$750–$1,500 per case (reactive) or $1,500/month for ongoing monitoring
Audience
Amazon sellers already on the platform with an active problem — not prospects, not fence-sitters
Why it works
Emotional relief outweighs rational ROI argument at this urgency level. One solved case builds a case study. High urgency compresses the sales cycle — founders pay for immediate relief, not future results.
AU specifics
GST confusion causing listing suppression; inactive listings stuck for 100+ days; reimbursement disputes calculated at supplier cost not selling price — all documented in AU seller forums as active, unresolved pain
Channel
Organic search + SEO content (50 Q&A content bank). Distressed sellers search for help — they do not scroll social. No paid ad spend required.
Risk
Operationally complex. Outcomes not fully controllable — Amazon support can drag. Scope must be clearly defined upfront: we escalate and manage, we do not guarantee reinstatement.
Recommendation

Sequencing by Audience

Which offer to use with each audience segment and campaign phase. Do not skip warm-up sequence.

Phase Audience Offer Channel
1 13 existing Phase 3 Ecom clients Free Brand Audit → Launch Sprint Direct outreach. No ad spend. Builds first 2 case studies.
2 Cold Meta — Audience 1 (Shopify/WooCommerce not yet on Amazon) 30-day guarantee or Readiness Audit as lead magnet Meta ads $100/day. Guarantee in primary creative; Readiness Audit as retargeting lead magnet.
3 Cold Meta — Audience 2 (Amazon seller, no PPC) Readiness Audit + 90-day pilot framing Meta retargeting. Audience 2 is burned — audit proves the gap; pilot reduces commitment anxiety.
4 Distressed AU Amazon sellers (stuck listings, account issues) Rescue Desk Organic search + SEO content (50 Q&A content bank). No paid ads required.
5 Cold email — ~700 leads (post case studies only) Founding Client Rate Lock Mailchimp. One-shot asset — do NOT deploy before 2 case studies exist. Rate lock creates urgency without discount.
Do not run revenue share or no-retainer pricing until 6+ months of operational cost-per-client data exists. Do not deploy cold email until 2 case studies exist. Add Rescue Desk as soon as the internal process is documented — this segment has high urgency and pays without long sales cycles.
