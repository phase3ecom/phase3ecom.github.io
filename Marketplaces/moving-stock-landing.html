Questions

Before you get moving

What's the difference between TX Amazon Launch and TX Marketplace Accelerator?+

TX Amazon Launch is one-time setup. TX Marketplace Accelerator includes launch plus ongoing management: listing optimisation, PPC, A+ Content, FBA monitoring, and eBay / Catch expansion.

I already have an online store. Do I need to change anything?+

No. Amazon runs alongside your existing store, not instead of it — it finds buyers who search directly on the platform.

What if I already have an Amazon seller account?+

We can work with existing accounts. We audit first to identify compliance issues, listing problems or account-health flags before building on top of it.

How does the 30-day guarantee work?+

TX Amazon Launch guarantees products listed and live within 30 days. TX Marketplace Accelerator guarantees a first sale within 30 days of going live, or money back. Both subject to product eligibility; T&Cs apply.

What products don't work on Amazon AU?+

Categories with heavy regulatory requirements, restricted listings, or low AU search volume need a case-by-case assessment — we flag these before starting.

How much should I budget for Amazon PPC?+

For a new seller with 5–10 SKUs, a realistic starting budget is $500–$1,500/month. PPC management is included in Accelerator; the ad spend is your budget, billed directly by Amazon.
