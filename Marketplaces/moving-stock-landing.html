Questions

Before you get moving

Isn't Amazon just going to undercut my own store? +

Marketplaces reach buyers who were never going to find your store — they search Amazon first. Done right, it adds a channel rather than cannibalising one, and the brand exposure feeds your direct sales too.

How long until we're live and seeing orders? +

Launch setup is measured in weeks, not months. Orders follow ranking and reviews, which compound — we tune continuously rather than promising an overnight spike.

We tried a freelancer and it stalled — why is this different? +

Marketplaces punish half-setups: compliance gaps, thin listings, no ad strategy. We run the whole system — setup, rank, ads, reviews, expansion — as one accountable process with monthly reporting.

What does it cost, and is it worth it for us? +

Pricing is set per scope on the call. If the audit shows the numbers won't work for your product and margins, we'll tell you — we only take stores we can actually move.

What's the cost of just leaving it another year? +

Every month off the marketplaces is search demand converting for a competitor and reviews they're banking that you'll later have to catch. The gap compounds against you.
