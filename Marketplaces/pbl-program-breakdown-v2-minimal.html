Research System — How PBL Finds What Works

Most creators skip research. PBL treats it as the foundation of everything.

📏
The 5x Rule: An outlier video = one that gets at least 5× more views than the account's total follower count. This is the only metric that matters for identifying what content to model. Ignore vanity metrics.

Daily vs. Per-Client Research

ActivityFrequencyPurpose
Intentional feed scrolling20 mins dailyPattern recognition — hooks, formats, topics performing now
Outlier mining per client10–12 hrs/monthIdentify 5x content in client's niche. Baked into every package.

📊 ViewStats
YouTube analytics tool. Mine standout thumbnails and titles, port visual hooks into short-form openings.

Section 05

Hook Engineering — The Most Important Skill

Priority Order — Every Piece of Content

🪝 Hook
📝 Script
🎬 Format
✂️ Editing

If the hook doesn't work, nothing else matters.

The 3-Layer Hook (First 3 Seconds)

HIGHEST WEIGHT
👁️
Layer 1
Visual
Something to SEE — movement, graphic, text reveal, physical action
📄
Layer 2
Text Overlay
Something to READ — the hook as on-screen text before Gary speaks
🔊
Layer 3
Audio / Verbal
Something to HEAR — Gary's voice delivering the hook out loud
⚠️ Visual carries the most weight. If forced to drop one layer, drop audio before visual. Never drop text overlay.

Hook Formulas That Work

Regret / Hindsight
"What I wish I knew about [X] at [age] instead of [later]"
→ Taps reflection, positions speaker as someone who learned the hard way
Contrast
"This is what happens when [unexpected condition]"
→ Creates curiosity, implies a reveal the viewer stays to see
Metric-Based
"I reviewed [number] [thing]. Here's what I found."
→ Authority + specificity + implies valuable data ahead
Direct Challenge
"You're [doing X wrong / leaving $X on table / missing Y]"
→ Pattern interrupt, mild discomfort, viewer stays to resolve
Story Opener
"Last [week], a [client] told me [surprising thing]"
→ Narrative pull — reader wants the resolution
Affordability / Access
"How to [valuable outcome] for [surprisingly low cost/time]"
→ Removes objection before viewer raises it

What to Avoid

🚫 Don't niche your hook like an ad — broad openers hold more viewers, niche the CTA not the hook.
🚫 Don't copy competitor value propositions — steal format and structure, add your own expertise.
🚫 Don't use AI to write the final hook — use AI for research and drafts, refine with human voice.

Section 06

Script Writing Framework

The 4-Part Script Document

#SectionWhat it contains
1️⃣Format & Filming NotesCamera angle, location, props, talking head vs b-roll, key visual instructions
2️⃣Bulleted Delivery LinesNot word-for-word. Bullet points Gary delivers in his own words — prevents stiff delivery
3️⃣CaptionCore value in plain text. Searchable. Includes keywords. Optimised for SEO/AEO
4️⃣Subtitle SpecificationKey lines to text-overlay on screen during edit. Same font as hook text.

The Narrative Arc — Every Script

01
Broad Opener
Maximum Watch Time
Relatable problem anyone might have. Holds as many viewers as possible in the first 5–10 seconds.
02
Narrow Value
Serve the Ideal Client
Specific insight only an ecom store owner would care about. Naturally filters to the right audience.
03
Niche CTA
Qualify the Lead
Highly specific action only the ideal client responds to. Qualifies before they DM or comment.
📖 Readability rule: Use Hemingway Editor. Target 5th grade reading level. Most audiences process short-form content passively — if it requires effort, they scroll. 15–30 seconds is the ideal Reel length for completion + rewatch.

Section 07

Content Formats — The 4 Templates

PBL uses four interchangeable format templates. When a script succeeds, re-film it across multiple formats to extract maximum reach from one idea.

01
Best for: authority, contrarian takes, personal story
🎤 Talking Head
Gary on camera, speaking directly to lens. No props, minimal movement. Highest perceived credibility. Most trust-building format. Edit with bold subtitle overlays, cut on rhythm, slight zoom for emphasis.
02
Best for: frameworks, step-by-step, comparisons
📋 Whiteboard / Text Reveal
Screen with a list, diagram, or text appearing progressively. Physical whiteboard or digital reveal in CapCut/Premiere. Gary may or may not appear. Timed text reveals synced to voiceover.
03
Best for: Q&A, myth-busting, client walk-throughs
🤝 Two-Person Dialogue
Gary with another person (team member, client, collaborator) in conversation. High engagement from natural back-and-forth. Edit with split-screen or alternating cuts, reaction shots.
04
Best for: behind-the-scenes, process, location
🎵 Audio B-Roll
Voiceover or music over B-roll footage. Gary does not appear. Easiest to batch — capture footage, voice over later. Text overlays carry the hook, footage supports the narrative.
♻️
Cloning Rule: When a script hits the 5x Rule, re-film across all 4 formats, swapping nouns (brand names, numbers, locations). One idea → 4+ pieces of content.

Section 08

Video Editing Style

Editing is 4th in priority. An average edit of a great hook outperforms a great edit of a bad hook every time. Don't let editing become the excuse for not posting.

Editing Stack

Premiere Pro  DaVinci Resolve  CapCut (mobile DIY)

Key Editing Rules

📝
Text Overlays
Bold, high-contrast text. Appears in first frame before Gary speaks. Minimum 3 seconds on screen. Centre 80% safe zone. Animated effects (fade, typewriter, slide) increase watch time.
💬
Subtitles
Always include — majority watch without sound. Match timing to speech precisely. Same font/style as text overlays for brand consistency.
⚡
Pacing
Cut on rhythm. Remove all pauses, filler words (um, uh, like). Slight zoom on key delivery lines. Hook must land in first 3 seconds — no slow intros.
🎞️
B-Roll
Layer over talking head when Gary references something visual. 2–3 second clips, not long pans. Supports narrative, doesn't compete with it.
🎵
Music / Audio
Trending audio = discoverability boost. Use IG's native audio library. Duck music to 10–20% under voiceover. No copyrighted music.
🎨
Visual Consistency
Consistent colour grade across all videos = faster brand recognition. Clean, well-lit shot preferred over heavy filter. 9:16 vertical always — never landscape.

Section 09

Stories Strategy — The Daily Cash Register

Stories are not optional. Reels build the audience. Stories convert it. Skipping Stories leaves the most qualified, warm leads untouched.

Daily Story Structure (3–5 Frames)

FrameContentPurpose
🖼️ Frame 1Before / After image (real photo, not text overlay)Visual proof. Builds desire. Most engaging frame type.
💭 Frame 2One tip or insight (short, single idea)Value delivery. Keeps the audience engaged daily.
👉 Frame 3CTA — link, keyword (DM me AUDIT), or pollLead capture. This is where the click or DM comes from.
📊 Performance benchmark at scale (529K–1M followers): ~500 clicks/day from Stories alone. This pipeline runs independently of Reel performance.

Story Rules

Before/after images outperform text-only Stories significantly. Always include a link or keyword trigger in at least one daily Story. Behind-the-scenes builds intimacy — show the work, the process. Don't overthink — Stories disappear in 24 hours. Volume beats polish here.

ManyChat Setup Notes

Set a comment keyword in each Reel caption: "Comment AUDIT below and I'll send you the link." When anyone comments that keyword, ManyChat auto-DMs them instantly.

Follower-gating option: Require follow before unlocking the DM. ManyChat shows a "Just followed" button — confirmation → delivers resource. Adds follower count alongside leads.

Lead Magnet Strategy

High-quality lead magnet signals service quality. Gate behind email collection — builds an email list independent of the algorithm. Ava's "1,000 hooks" freebie took ~300 hours to build. Quality of free content reflects quality of paid service.

Don't create from scratch. Reverse-engineer what already works, then add your expertise on top.

Day 3
✍️ Script
  • Write 15–30 scripts using proven structures
  • Apply Broad → Narrow → Niche arc
  • Run each through Hemingway
  • Write captions + keyword for each
Day 4
🎥 Film
  • Film 5 talking-head versions
  • Film 5 alternate format versions
  • Consistent background > perfect setup
  • Batch everything in one session
Day 5
⚡ ManyChat Live
  • Set up ManyChat account
  • Configure first keyword (AUDIT/FREE)
  • Write auto-DM sequence (1–2 msgs)
  • Build proof landing page or Linktree
🚀 Days 6–7: Post and measure. Post first 1–2 Reels. Post 3–5 Story frames daily. Measure after 48 hours — which hook got closest to 5x views? Double down on that topic and format in the next batch.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Mistake
Buying followers
Why it fails
Corrupts algorithmic distribution. May require full account restart.
✅ Fix
Organic only. Always. No shortcuts.
❌ Mistake
Niching hook like an ad
Why it fails
Shrinks audience before value is delivered — the hook filters before the content hooks.
✅ Fix
Broad opener → narrow value → niche CTA
❌ Mistake
Copying competitor value
Why it fails
Same thing said by two people = both are diluted. No differentiation.
✅ Fix
Steal format + hook structure. Add original expertise only.
❌ Mistake
AI writes final script
Why it fails
Generic language, loses authentic voice — viewer senses it immediately.
✅ Fix
AI for research + structure. Human refinement for voice.
❌ Mistake
Posting without a CTA
Why it fails
Views don't become leads without a capture mechanism. Audience grows, revenue doesn't.
✅ Fix
Every Reel has comment keyword. Every Story has link.
❌ Mistake
Skipping Stories
Why it fails
Reels build audience. Stories convert it. No Stories = no pipeline from warm followers.
✅ Fix
Daily Stories non-negotiable once Reels are live.
