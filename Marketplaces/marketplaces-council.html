A
Threat / Defence
A

“Your brand is already on Amazon. The question is whether you control it.”

LARGE ECOM OWNER
This lands. I’ve built this brand for a decade and I’ve never once checked what’s on Amazon under my brand name. If a reseller is out there with wrong images, wrong price, 2-star reviews — that’s real reputational damage I’m not aware of. What makes me act: show me what’s actually there right now. Do the search in front of me.
SIDE HUSTLE OWNER
This scares me a little, but I wonder if it’s a scare tactic. My products are probably too niche to attract resellers. But if you could show me specifically — not generically — that it’s happening, I’d take it seriously.
EXISTING AMAZON SELLER
This doesn’t apply to me directly — I’m already there. But it reminds me that other sellers ARE running sponsored ads on my category terms, and I’m sitting there organic. The ‘control’ framing applies to PPC too.
SCEPTICAL CFO
This creates anxiety without a dollar value attached. ‘Control your brand’ is not an ROI statement. What does a hijacked listing actually cost in lost revenue? Show me the number.
EXISTING PHASE 3 CLIENT
I trust Phase 3. If Will tells me my brand is being misrepresented on Amazon, I believe him — he’s never oversold me. The threat lands harder because it’s coming from someone I already trust.
CROSSTALK The CFO and Side Hustle Owner converge: threat-only is not enough — it must be made specific and quantifiable. The Large Owner and Phase 3 Client are most responsive, for different reasons (reputation risk vs trusted source). The Amazon Seller reframes it usefully: ‘control’ applies to PPC visibility, not just listing ownership.
VERDICT Best cold outreach hook — but only if delivered as a live demonstration, not a generic claim.
B
Identity / Builder’s Pride
B

“You built something real. Don’t let someone else own your name on the biggest search engine in the country.”

LARGE ECOM OWNER
This hits harder than expected. I’ve spent years building brand equity. Someone else occupying my brand name on a platform with 60% AU buyer penetration — and profiting from the equity I built — is genuinely offensive.
SIDE HUSTLE OWNER
Honestly? I don’t fully identify as a ‘brand builder’ yet. My business is real but I still think of it as a side hustle. This might not land for me unless you show me it already matters at my scale.
EXISTING AMAZON SELLER
I feel this — I built my listings, I wrote my copy, and now competitors run sponsored ads above my organic result on my own product searches.
SCEPTICAL CFO
Identity doesn’t pay wages. ‘Brand dignity’ isn’t a line on a P&L.
EXISTING PHASE 3 CLIENT
Resonates. We’ve had conversations about brand consistency across channels. This fits how we already think about the business.
CROSSTALK Sharply divides by business maturity and identity. Works for Large Owner and Phase 3 Client; actively excludes the Side Hustle Owner; the CFO rejects it.
VERDICT Powerful one-on-one closing angle, not a mass campaign angle. Excludes too large a segment of the addressable market.
C
Single SKU Proof of Concept
C

“Test one product. 90 days. Real numbers. No full commitment.”

LARGE ECOM OWNER
I like the logic, but I’m slightly put off. At my scale, a ‘test’ feels like you don’t have conviction in your own service. I want a partner who knows what they’re doing, not one who needs to prove it to me.
SIDE HUSTLE OWNER
This is the only angle that makes me actually consider it. My biggest fear is committing $1k/month to something that doesn’t work. ‘Start with your best product, 90 days, see what happens, then decide’ — that’s a conversation I can have.
EXISTING AMAZON SELLER
This is exactly what I need. I’ve been burned by PPC before. If you said ‘one campaign on your top SKU, 90 days, defined budget, defined success metric’ — I’m in. I need a guardrail, not a blank cheque.
SCEPTICAL CFO
Most financially rational angle. Bounded experiment with measurable outcomes before committing to ongoing cost. I want clear KPIs defined before day one.
EXISTING PHASE 3 CLIENT
This is how Phase 3 already works with us — they’ve never oversold scope. Starting small and proving value before expanding is exactly their style.
CROSSTALK The only angle all five personas can engage with. Divides on what ‘proof of concept’ signals: Large Owner reads it as lack of confidence; everyone else reads it as responsible risk management.
VERDICT Best offer structure for reducing barriers, especially for Audience 2 (burned sellers) and the Side Hustle Owner. Should be the standard entry point.
D
Anti-Agency Positioning
D

“We’re not selling you Amazon. We’re solving the Amazon problem so you can stop thinking about it.”

LARGE ECOM OWNER
I’ve been pitched by Amazon agencies before and they all sound the same. This sounds different — and that’s valuable. I don’t want to think about Amazon. I want someone to handle it and tell me when something matters.
SIDE HUSTLE OWNER
‘Stop thinking about it’ is literally the dream. I have 47 things to think about. If you can remove one of them — specifically the ‘should I be on Amazon?’ anxiety I’ve had for two years — I’ll pay for that peace of mind almost regardless of whether Amazon itself is profitable.
EXISTING AMAZON SELLER
Less relevant to me — I’m already on Amazon. ‘We’ll manage it so you can stop obsessing’ is the right reframe for my version.
SCEPTICAL CFO
Suspicious. ‘Stop thinking about it’ is not a business outcome. Positioning as ‘not an Amazon agency’ when you are running Amazon services is a misdirection. I want to know exactly what’s being delivered.
EXISTING PHASE 3 CLIENT
This is exactly how Phase 3 already operates for us. I don’t think about Klaviyo or Google Ads bidding. Phase 3 handles it and flags when I need to make a decision. Extending that to Amazon feels completely natural.
CROSSTALK Phase 3 Client and Side Hustle Owner strongly moved. Large Owner intrigued by differentiation. CFO resistant — needs specific deliverables alongside the positioning.
VERDICT Best brand/category differentiator in the competitive set. Should be the underlying tone of everything, not a single campaign angle. Pair with concrete deliverables to satisfy the CFO.
E
Exit / Asset Value
E

“Amazon presence increases your business sale multiple. This is an investment in what your business is worth.”

LARGE ECOM OWNER
This is the only angle that made me sit up straight. I am actively thinking about exit. I didn’t know Amazon presence was a variable in valuation, but it makes complete sense — a buyer wants diversified, resilient revenue. If Phase 3 can show me data on what a controlled Amazon presence does to EBITDA multiples, this is a board-level conversation.
SIDE HUSTLE OWNER
Exit? I’m just trying to survive. This feels like it’s for someone else’s business. I’m not thinking about selling — I’m thinking about whether I can afford another $1k/month. This angle loses me completely.
EXISTING AMAZON SELLER
Slightly interesting, but not my primary concern. I care about this month’s profit, not a theoretical exit in 5 years.
SCEPTICAL CFO
Finally — a financial argument. I want to see actual data: what premium do acquirers pay for brands with established Amazon channels vs Shopify-only? If that data is credible, this is a compelling ROI case. $1k/month to increase a $2M business’s exit value by 20% is not a hard calculation.
EXISTING PHASE 3 CLIENT
I’ve never thought about this. But now that you’ve said it, I can’t un-think it. If adding Amazon increases what we can sell for, I should know that. This is a strategic conversation, not a service upsell.
CROSSTALK Sharply divides by business stage. Large Owner and CFO strongly engaged; Side Hustle Owner completely excluded; Existing Amazon Seller neutral.
VERDICT Highest-leverage angle for Large Owners and exit-aware clients. Use it as a closer in discovery calls, not as a cold hook. Narrow audience but high conversion when it lands.
06
Overall Synthesis
06

No single angle works across all five personas. The panel splits sharply by business stage. Use this to guide message sequencing, not message selection.

Key Findings
  • ✓ No single angle works across all five personas. The panel splits sharply by business stage.
  • ✓ Phase 3 existing clients are the easiest win and are being underutilised. Every angle lands for them because of pre-existing trust. Upsell existing clients first.
  • ✓ Threat/Defence is the best cold outreach hook — but only if made specific. A generic claim gets dismissed. A live demo converts the same message into something undeniable.
  • ✓ Single SKU PoC removes more barriers than any other angle — especially for Audience 2 and margin-anxious owners. Should be standard offer structure.
  • ✓ Exit/Asset is a closer for Large Owners, not an opener. Use after trust is established, not in cold creative.
  • ✓ Anti-Agency positioning is the brand differentiator. Not an angle — the underlying tone of everything.
Recommended Sequencing
# Step Rationale
1 Lead with Threat/Defence Best cold hook when delivered as a live demonstration
2 Deliver Anti-Agency as the brand tone Not an angle — the consistent underlying voice
3 Convert with Single SKU PoC as the offer structure Removes commitment anxiety for the widest audience
4 Close Large Owners with Exit/Asset in the discovery call High conversion when it lands — but requires trust first
5 Win existing Phase 3 clients first Pre-existing trust makes every angle work
