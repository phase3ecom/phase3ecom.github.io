Rescue / Distressed Segment (v3 — New)

Rescue

Has an active crisis: inactive listing stuck for weeks, billing lockout, compliance loop, reimbursement dispute, account health warning, or Featured Offer lost to a hijacker. High urgency. High willingness to pay for relief. No long sales cycle.

Audience Rule

Target the “already decided” buyer — they’ve made the decision, just haven’t executed. Do not spend paid budget trying to convince fence-sitters. The primary campaign assumes Amazon conviction exists; it sells TX Marketplace Accelerator as the fastest, lowest-risk vehicle to execute on a decision already made.

The Rescue segment is not acquired via paid ads — it is acquired via organic search (SEO content targeting “stuck listing Amazon AU”, “Amazon account locked Australia”, etc.) and word of mouth. High intent, no nurture required.

Guarantee
04
Current Guarantee — v3 (Marketplace Offer)
“We guarantee to have your products live on Amazon within 30 days of kickoff, or your $1,500 setup fee is fully refunded.”
Short form: “We guarantee to have your products live within 30 days, or your money back.”
✓ Use
  • State once, near the end of any script or page section
  • Deliver as a matter of fact — not as salesmanship
  • Frame as proof of confidence: “Because we’ve done this enough times to know how it ends.”
  • Use short form in ad scripts; long form in landing page and blog
  • Pair with a specific dollar figure ($1,500) when space allows
✗ Never Do
  • Lead with the guarantee — it’s a trust closer, not a hook
  • Use desperate framing: “We’re so confident we GUARANTEE...”
  • Imply a sales or revenue guarantee — this guarantees listing delivery only
  • Use old v1 language: “guarantee to double your sales in 90 days”
  • Use “work for free” framing — the refund language is the v2 standard
What The Guarantee Covers
Scope of Guarantee

The guarantee covers listing delivery — products live and visible on Amazon AU within 30 days of kickoff. It does not cover sales velocity, ad performance, review count, or organic ranking. This is intentional: Phase 3 controls delivery. Phase 3 does not control buyer behaviour, Amazon’s algorithm, or market demand.

The refund is the $1,500 setup fee. Ongoing retainer fees (Month 2+) are not covered by the launch guarantee — they are tied to ongoing management performance, not initial delivery.

Strategic Role of the Guarantee
Why It Works

The guarantee does not reduce risk for the buyer — it signals expertise. A team that guarantees 30-day delivery has done this enough times to know the failure modes. The willingness to refund is not desperation; it’s confidence made concrete.

Position it last in every piece of content, after the audience has seen the problem (invisible customers), the cost of inaction (review compounding), and the solution (TX Marketplace Accelerator). The guarantee is the final proof point, not the opening argument.

The Market Gap
