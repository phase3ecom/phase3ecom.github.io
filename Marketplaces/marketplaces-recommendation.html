Recommended Approach
01
The Single Recommended Approach
Control, then grow.
The Logic

Lead with control (brand defence, competitive threat, listing ownership). Deliver proof of concept (single SKU, 90 days, defined metrics). Then grow (full catalogue, ongoing retainer, exit asset building).

Position Phase 3 not as an Amazon agency, but as the partner that makes sure your brand is protected and profitable wherever your customers are looking — including Amazon.

What This IS and Is NOT
WHAT THIS IS NOT
  • A pitch to sign up for Amazon management
  • An education campaign about why Amazon is growing
  • A “more channels = more revenue” argument
  • An agency services catalogue
WHAT THIS IS
  • A brand protection service with a measurable proof of concept
  • A trust extension of an existing Phase 3 relationship
  • A defence against something that’s already happening without the client’s knowledge
  • The lowest-risk, most credible entry into Amazon for an AU ecommerce SME
02
Offer Structure
02
TIER 1 — AMAZON BRAND AUDIT
Free
Search the prospect’s brand name on Amazon AU. Document what’s there: resellers, listing quality, review score, competitor ads on their brand terms. Present findings in a 20-minute call. This is the live threat demo. Cost to Phase 3: 1–2 hours. The free audit is the real acquisition mechanism — it converts better than any ad because the prospect sees their own problem in real time.
TIER 2 — SETUP
$1,500 flat
Account creation, brand registry, GS1/GTINs, listing build for top 3 SKUs, FBA configuration, policy compliance. Deliverable: live on Amazon AU, correctly. Timeline: 2–3 weeks.
TIER 3 — 90-DAY PILOT
$1,000/month · 3-month minimum
PPC management (Sponsored Products), listing optimisation, monthly reporting with defined KPIs agreed at kickoff (target ACoS, units moved, search rank improvement). At day 90: review results, renew or exit cleanly. No lock-in beyond the pilot.
TIER 4 — ONGOING RETAINER
$1,000/month · rolling
Earned by pilot results. Full PPC management, catalogue expansion, quarterly strategy review.

Lead Message
03
Cold Outreach Hook (Email + Meta Ad)

“Search your brand name on Amazon right now.”

[pause / visual]

“Whatever you find — that’s your brand’s reputation on a platform 60% of Australians buy from. We’ll tell you exactly what’s there, and what it’s costing you. Free. No pitch.”

CTA: “Get your free Amazon brand audit.”
Not “book a call.” The audit is the product that sells the setup. Reframes the entire entry point from “buy our service” to “see your own problem.”

Key Strategic Principles
PRINCIPLE 01
Sequencing
Existing clients → audits → case studies → cold list → scale ads. Not the other way around.
PRINCIPLE 02
The Audit Is the Product
It sells itself. Don’t skip straight to pitching the retainer.
PRINCIPLE 03
Anti-Agency Tone Throughout
“We’re not an Amazon agency” — say this explicitly. This is the category differentiator.
PRINCIPLE 04
90-Day Pilot Removes the Biggest Barrier
Commitment anxiety kills more deals than price does.
PRINCIPLE 05
Exit/Asset Is a Closer, Not an Opener
Use it in discovery calls with owners who mention exit or scale.
PRINCIPLE 06
Never Lead with Growth
Lead with control, defence, and protection. Growth is the outcome, not the pitch.
