09
FAQ
09
Is this right for my brand?
Best fit: Shopify or WooCommerce brands with at least 5 SKUs and $300k+ annual revenue. Products must be suitable for Amazon AU’s category requirements (we’ll confirm this in your application).
What if I already have an Amazon account?
We audit and rebuild from there. Existing sellers with flat sales are a common intake — we assess what’s blocking growth before we touch anything.
What happens after Month 1?
Month 1 is your launch. Month 2 onward is the growth phase — review strategy, pricing, early ads. Month 4+ is when catalogue expansion and cross-marketplace work begins. You’re buying a programme, not a task.
Can you add eBay AU?
Yes, from Month 4 as part of the Compound phase. We scope it separately so there’s no confusion about what the initial retainer covers.
