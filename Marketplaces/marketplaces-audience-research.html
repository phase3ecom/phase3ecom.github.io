01
Shopify / WooCommerce Sellers
01
Why They Haven't Done It

These are the verbatim objections from Shopify and WooCommerce sellers who are NOT on Amazon. The fear underneath the fear: “I’ll spend money and time getting set up and nothing will happen.”

“It’s too complicated — I got lost in Seller Central and gave up”
“I don’t want Amazon to copy my products”
“The fees kill your margins — 10–15% referral fee on top of FBA”
“Amazon can just kick you off without warning”
“I don’t own the customer relationship — no email, no data”
“I’m already drowning running my Shopify store”
“I don’t even know if my products are a good fit”
“Amazon will just undercut me on my own products”
“Even items sold by Amazon themselves are overpriced vs local stores — so what’s the point?”
What They Don't Know
  • 60% of Australians purchased from Amazon in the past 12 months. They are invisible to that traffic.
  • 66% of Australians have Prime access. Amazon is mainstream, not a niche.
  • 48% of shoppers visit a brand’s own website after discovering it on Amazon first. Amazon is a customer acquisition channel for your Shopify store — not a competitor to it.
  • Unauthorised third-party resellers may already be selling their products on Amazon — without their control over pricing, listing quality, or brand rep.
  • Amazon accounted for nearly 10% of all online shopping in Australia in 2024. Projected to double by 2026.
  • Amazon is now a top-3 product search engine in Australia — people use it to find products, not just to buy things they’ve already decided on.

02
Amazon Sellers Not Running PPC
02
Why They're Not Running Ads

These are objections from Amazon sellers who have an account but are NOT running PPC. The reality behind the hesitation: Australia’s lower search volume means keyword signals take 60–90 days to stabilise. Sellers who try PPC without strategy burn budget early and conclude “PPC doesn’t work here.”

“I tried PPC once and spent $500 with nothing to show for it”
“I’m already getting organic sales — why would I pay for ads?”
“Amazon PPC is confusing — too many campaign types, too many levers”
“In Australia the market’s too small, the data’s too thin for ads to work”
“I don’t have time to manage it properly”
“Chinese sellers will just undercut me anyway — what’s the point?”
“I’ve grown 30% every year — but this year I’m flat and I don’t know why”
“PPC just burns money if you don’t know what you’re doing”
“I’d rather just lower my price than run ads”
“The fees are already killing me — I can’t afford ads on top”
What They Don't Know
  • Without PPC, competitors ARE running Sponsored ads against their listings and stealing Buy Box visibility
  • “In Australia, PPC is about efficiency not aggression” — lower competition = lower CPCs when run correctly
  • PPC data reveals what customers are actually searching — organic keyword optimisation without it is guesswork
  • Listing suppression happens silently; PPC is often the first signal something’s wrong with a listing
  • A well-managed AU campaign should be profitable within 60–90 days
