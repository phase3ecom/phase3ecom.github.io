Reduce the mental load and uncertainty customers feel before purchase. When the product feels easier to understand, transport, and install, the customer believes the whole job will be easier.

Guided decision-making replaces open-ended browsing. FenceStay’s role is to move customers from “I’m not sure what to buy” to “I know exactly what I need to order.”

04
New Product Launch
04

FenceStay is launching a powered post driver — a tow hitch-mounted, car battery-powered solution for driving large posts into hard ground. Three and a half years in development. The original 27/05 target has moved: the first unit is being manufactured the week of 02/06, with photography and demo video to follow. Revised launch target: late June 2026.

  • 5–10 units per month at ~$5,000–$6,000 RRP adds $27,500–$55,000/month in additional revenue
  • Combined with existing fence products, $100,000/month is achievable in Stage 1
  • Opens a new high-value buyer segment: the lifestyle property owner
Why This Product Wins
SolutionCostLimitation
Manual post driver~$300–$50020kg, requires two people, exhausting in hard or rocky ground
Petrol / pneumatic driver$2,000–$3,500Unreliable in hard ground, ongoing maintenance overhead
Tractor-mounted driver$20,000+Requires tractor ownership, overkill for smaller jobs
FenceStay Powered DriverTBC (~$5,000–$6,000)Car battery-powered, portable, effective in hard ground — no extra equipment needed
