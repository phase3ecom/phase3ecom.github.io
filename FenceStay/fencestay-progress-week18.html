06 · Landing Pages (for review today)

Audience landing pages: ready for review

Three persona-specific pages built on one shared template, for paid-ad traffic. All three (Farmers, Contractor, and Small Acreage / DIY) are built with dedicated real photography per persona and staged on a draft theme, ready for your review and sign-off today.

  • ✓Three pages built with curated real photos matched to each audience: Farmers, Contractor, and Small Acreage / DIY.
  • ✓Staged on a draft theme for sign-off, ready to publish once approved.
Improvements we're making to lift conversion
  • ▸One clear primary action per page, sending visitors straight to the right starter kit rather than a general browse page.
  • ▸Fence Calculator repositioned as a guided assist that funnels into the right kit, not a step that sends people off the page.
  • ▸Trust cues at every call-to-action: Australian made, proven equivalent strength, 30-day returns, and customer reviews placed next to the buy button.
  • ▸Conversion tracking on every action before we scale spend, so each dollar's path from ad to page to sale is measured.
  • ▸Faster mobile load and a clearer first screen, so the offer and call-to-action are visible without scrolling.
  • ▸A "not sure it suits your place?" confidence step that routes hesitant buyers to a quick fit-check rather than off the page.
To confirm in this meeting
Sign off the three pages to publish, and approve the conversion improvements before we point paid spend at them.
Tracking gate
Landing page conversion events go live before we scale ad spend, so every dollar's path from ad to page to sale is measured.
