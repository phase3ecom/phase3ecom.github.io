Section 02

Brand Strategy

Brand Foundations

Brand Pillars

Pillar 01
The Job Feels Easier Already

Reduce the mental load and uncertainty customers feel before purchase. When the product feels easier to understand, transport, and install, the customer believes the whole job will be easier.

Pillar 02
Buy the Right Setup the First Time

Guided decision-making replaces open-ended browsing. FenceStay's role is to move customers from "I'm not sure what to buy" to "I know exactly what I need to order."

Pillar 03
Faster, Smarter, Proven in Real Conditions

The rational case for FenceStay over traditional methods: faster installation, reduced labour, simpler transport, suitable for real Australian conditions.

Core Message
"Build it faster. Without the hassle."

Website Audit — Gaps to Address
  1. Shop by Job — Missing The brand strategy identifies "Shop by Job" as a primary navigation item and a key homepage section. It is the main entry point for customers who are thinking about a job, not a product. Currently absent from the site navigation and homepage.
  2. Trade / Bulk Pathway — Missing The Contractor / Trade Buyer persona has no dedicated entry point in the navigation or on the homepage. Trade buyers need to find bulk packs and trade pricing quickly — the current site has no visible route.
  3. Hero CTA Routing — Incorrect The current hero CTAs are "Use the Fencestay Calculator" and "Shop Kits & Bundles." The brand strategy specifies "Use the Fence Calculator | Shop by Job." The second CTA should route to the job-based buying pathway, not the kits collection.
  4. Intent Segmentation Block — Missing The homepage was designed to include a "Shop Your Way" block immediately after the hero — a four-way segment (Help me choose / Shop by job / Shop products / Trade & Bulk) to route users by intent and reduce friction from the first scroll.
  5. Earthy Colour Palette — Under-Applied Deep Olive (#556B4F) and Earth Brown (#8A5A3C) are not visibly applied on the site. Dark Blue (#2F5BA6) is dominant. This makes the site feel more corporate than the brand guidelines intend.
  6. Audience-Specific Landing Pages — Do Not Exist The brand strategy and paid media plan both depend on landing pages tailored to each persona. Without these, all ad traffic lands on the homepage.
  7. How It Works — Homepage Section Only For paid search and Meta awareness ads targeting people researching fencing solutions, this needs to be a standalone landing page with a direct conversion path at the end.

International Expansion

Prerequisites

  • $100k/mo sustained for 2 consecutive months — Proof the AU market is a reliable revenue base
  • Audience LPs and messaging validated — International LPs will be adapted from AU — they need to be proven first
  • Bundles and kits structure locked — Marketplace + international catalogues require standardised product architecture
  • Email flows fully operational — Retention infrastructure must be solid before expanding acquisition
  • Creative asset library built (FGC + UGC) — International ads require proven creative — test in AU first
  • AU marketplace exploration complete — Validates catalogue structure before US/NZ expansion

Recommended Sequence

  1. Research: NZ first (closest market, same language, similar agriculture), then UK
  2. Test: Marketplace listings (Amazon AU/NZ or eBay) before standalone store
  3. Expand: Dedicated international Shopify store once NZ/UK marketplace validates demand
