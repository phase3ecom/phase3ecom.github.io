Frequency has risen from 2.41 in December to 3.85 in April — a 60% increase. When frequency exceeds 3.5–4.0 on a retargeting audience, CTR drops and CPA rises. The February dip (4 purchases at 7x ROAS) coincides with frequency hitting 3.5. New creative is needed to refresh the audience pool before fatigue further impacts performance.

Zero Campaigns — Largest Untapped Opportunity
No email campaigns have ever been sent to the 2,365-strong subscriber list. 100% of Klaviyo revenue is generated by automated flows alone. A regular campaign cadence (2–4/month) to existing subscribers costs nothing in media spend and could realistically generate $30,000–$60,000+ in additional annual revenue based on industry benchmarks for this AOV range.

Welcome Series — Upgrade Opportunity
The welcome series has strong open rates (56–72%) but generates near-zero revenue. The current emails are brand-weak ("Thanks for subscribing!" / "Follow us on social"). These should be upgraded to introduce the mill range, explain the benefits of fresh-ground flour, and include a first-purchase incentive. High-intent subscribers dropping off the welcome series are a directly recoverable revenue source.
Post-Purchase — Cross-Sell Not Wired
The Post-Purchase Review flow has a 33.7% open rate — high engagement — but earns $0. The subject line focuses on reviews, not repeat purchase or accessories. Adding a cross-sell step (stone replacement sets, cereal flaker combos, accessories) to this flow could convert existing buyers into higher-LTV customers at low effort.

GA4 Tracking Gap — Audit Required
GA4 is recording 184 transactions and $191,925 in revenue. WooCommerce backend shows 378 orders and $411,872 in gross revenue over the same period. GA4 is only capturing approximately 49% of actual transactions. This means channel attribution, conversion rates, and landing page data in GA4 are significantly underreported. A GA4 tracking audit is recommended — likely causes include ad blockers, tracking code placement, or checkout flow events not firing correctly.
